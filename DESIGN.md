# Developer studio

This revision replaces the warm editorial direction with a dark, technical studio. A large sans-serif headline and original SVG wireframe establish the opening. Work appears immediately after the hero as an expandable project index, rather than a card gallery. The remaining sections use structural grids, crisp borders, and a cobalt accent. Light mode remains available and theme selection persists.

The geometric project covers are original abstract SVG artwork. Saved project images take precedence. No mock product screenshots, fabricated metrics, or borrowed portfolio assets are used. Preview content is labeled; real Supabase content and the existing admin workflows remain supported.

Manrope and IBM Plex Mono are self-hosted with their OFL licenses in `public/fonts`. Controls support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, and narrow screens. Project disclosures expose their expanded state and work with the technology filters.

The previous iteration researched published source from Brittany Chiang, Tania Rascia, and Paco Coursey. This revision abandons its editorial composition and does not reproduce those layouts. Live-site access was blocked in the research environment; no claim is made to have evaluated current live versions or rankings.
